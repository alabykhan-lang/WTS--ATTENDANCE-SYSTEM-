# Student digital register

The student register is generated from active Central Registry pupils and their current class placement. It has separate `morning` and `afternoon` records for every school day.

An authorised recorder can load a class, mark all present, adjust exceptions, save, review incomplete rows, confirm and print. Confirmation locks the register from casual edits. Reopening requires Attendance administrator authority, a reason and an audit record.

Supported interpretation labels include Present, Absent, Late, Excused, Official activity, Sick leave, Early departure, Half day, School activity, Not expected, School closed and Incomplete. School closure and not-expected periods are not counted as pupil absence.

The printable view is designed for A4 landscape, repeated headings, readable black-and-white output, signature space and page-safe rows. Ministry-specific symbols remain configurable; the system does not invent terminology.
